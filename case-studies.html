HOSPITALITY / PRODUCT EXPERIENCE

Taking a hotel-focused product to market.

Hotels coordinate guest enquiries, front-desk activity and service teams in a fast-moving environment. We took a hotel-focused product to market with the goal of making everyday hotel operations more organised through a practical digital workflow.

The business problem

Information and requests can move between people and departments, making it harder to maintain a consistent guest experience.

Product direction

Bring key operational steps into a clearer shared workflow, helping teams stay aligned around guest needs and day-to-day tasks.

What this demonstrates

Product thinking grounded in a specific industry, translating operational context into a usable digital product.
